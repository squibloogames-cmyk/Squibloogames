Request Account & Data Deletion

SQUIBLOO GAMES provides a way for SUM THREADS players to request deletion of their account and associated game data.

You can also use the DELETE ACCOUNT & DATA option available inside the SUM THREADS app.

Important: Account and data deletion may permanently remove your SUM THREADS cloud progress, leaderboard entry, anonymous account, and locally stored game data. Deleted game data may not be recoverable.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to:

squibloogames@gmail.com
REQUEST DATA DELETION

Please use the subject:

SUM THREADS Account Deletion Request

If available, include your SUM THREADS player ID or Firebase anonymous user ID so that we can identify the correct game data. Do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

What Will Be Deleted

Depending on the circumstances and applicable requirements, the deletion process is intended to remove the player's associated SUM THREADS data, which may include:

  • Firebase Authentication account;
  • SUM THREADS player data stored in Firestore;
  • public leaderboard entry;
  • game progress and progression data;
  • virtual economy and booster data;
  • profile display name and avatar;
  • locally stored game data on the device.

Third-Party Services

SUM THREADS uses third-party services such as Google Firebase and Google Mobile Ads. Some information processed by third-party providers may be subject to their own retention policies and legal obligations.

For more information, please review our Privacy Policy.

Deletion Requests

SQUIBLOO GAMES will review deletion requests and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law and our Privacy Policy.

We may need sufficient information to verify and identify the relevant game data before processing a request.
