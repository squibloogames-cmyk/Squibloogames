SUM THREADS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SUM THREADS ("SUM THREADS", the "Game", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the SUM THREADS mobile game.

This policy describes the current implementation of the Game and explains the categories of information that may be handled by SQUIBLOO GAMES and the third-party services used by the Game.

1. Information We Handle

Anonymous Account Identifier

SUM THREADS uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication. This creates a Firebase user identifier (UID) associated with your cloud game data.

Profile Information

You may choose a display name and avatar. Your selected display name and avatar may be stored with your player data and may appear on public leaderboards.

Please do not use sensitive or personally identifying information as your display name.

Gameplay and Progression

The Game may store information such as:

  • completed levels;
  • star ratings;
  • world and level progression;
  • high scores.

Virtual Economy

The Game may store:

  • coin balance;
  • lives;
  • life-regeneration information;
  • booster inventory;
  • daily-reward information;
  • equipped cosmetic or skin information where applicable.

Settings and Local App Information

The Game may store information locally on your device, including:

  • sound and music settings;
  • haptic settings;
  • accessibility settings such as colourblind mode;
  • notification preferences;
  • profile display name and avatar;
  • local game progress and economy state;
  • a locally generated analytics identifier;
  • legal acknowledgement state.

Analytics

SUM THREADS uses Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay and app usage.

Analytics events may include:

  • app opens;
  • level lifecycle events;
  • world and level navigation;
  • booster use;
  • lives and coins;
  • shop interactions;
  • rewarded advertisements;
  • banner interactions;
  • leaderboard interactions;
  • settings interactions.

Analytics events may contain gameplay parameters such as player identifiers, level information, scores, reward types, amounts, and other gameplay context.

Crash and Diagnostic Information

SUM THREADS uses Firebase Crashlytics for crash and diagnostic reporting. Depending on the diagnostic event, information may include crash or stack-trace data, device model, and Android operating-system information.

Advertising Information

SUM THREADS uses Google Mobile Ads for banner and rewarded advertising.

Google advertising services may process information associated with advertising, including advertising identifiers and information about ad requests, impressions, and interactions, subject to Google's applicable policies and your privacy choices.

Rewarded-Ad Verification

Rewarded advertisements may use Google AdMob Server-Side Verification (SSV).

The Game may send an anonymous Firebase user identifier and reward-related information for verification of rewarded-ad transactions. Validated rewarded-ad transactions may be recorded in the Game's Firestore data.

2. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

  • providing and maintaining game functionality;
  • saving and synchronizing gameplay progress;
  • maintaining leaderboards;
  • providing virtual rewards and game features;
  • measuring and understanding game usage;
  • diagnosing crashes and technical problems;
  • securing the Game and preventing abuse or manipulation;
  • delivering and measuring advertisements;
  • verifying rewarded-ad transactions;
  • maintaining and improving the Game.

3. Leaderboards

If you participate in a leaderboard, your selected display name, avatar, and score may be visible to other players.

Leaderboard information is stored in the Game's cloud database and is intended to be publicly queryable as part of the leaderboard feature.

Do not enter personal or sensitive information as your display name.

4. Third-Party Services

SUM THREADS currently uses Google services including:

  • Firebase Authentication;
  • Cloud Firestore;
  • Firebase Analytics;
  • Firebase Crashlytics;
  • Firebase Remote Config;
  • Firebase App Check;
  • Google Mobile Ads;
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).

These services may process information according to their applicable terms and privacy documentation.

Google's privacy information is available at:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

Where applicable, SUM THREADS uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and AdMob Privacy & Messaging mechanisms to manage advertising-related privacy choices.

For users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, applicable Google consent mechanisms may request choices concerning advertising-related processing. The applicable message and choices can depend on the user's region and existing consent status.

SUM THREADS provides a Privacy & Ad Choices control where supported so applicable users can revisit privacy choices.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, maintain game progress and leaderboards, operate and secure the service, comply with applicable obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain legitimate business records.

Retention periods can differ by data type and third-party service. Where information is no longer needed for the relevant purpose, it may be deleted or anonymized subject to applicable requirements.

7. Data Deletion

SUM THREADS provides an in-app DELETE ACCOUNT & DATA function.

The current implementation is designed to delete the player's Firestore player document, public leaderboard entry, Firebase Authentication account, and local game data.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups, logs, security records, or third-party systems where retention is necessary for legitimate operational, security, legal, or compliance purposes.

8. Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information. These may include rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or otherwise control certain processing.

The availability and scope of these rights depend on applicable law and the circumstances of processing.

You can use the in-app deletion functionality where available. For requests that cannot be handled through the Game, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

SUM THREADS is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in circumstances where this is not permitted, contact us so the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, deleted.

The appropriate age-related treatment and advertising configuration should be determined based on the final intended audience and applicable legal requirements.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information and the Game's services.

These measures include Firebase Authentication, Firestore security rules, Firebase App Check, and server-side verification mechanisms for rewarded advertisements where applicable.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

11. International Processing

Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where applicable, such processing is subject to safeguards and requirements imposed by applicable privacy laws and relevant service providers.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game, its data practices, third-party services, or applicable requirements change.

The updated version will be published at the Privacy Policy URL and/or made available through the Game.
